The summer of 2021 had an intense fire season in the Sierra Nevada region. On this day, the air quality was low and the air was overcast throughout the drive from Merced and across the mountains to the easter side. The entire region had a scifi vibe to it. Look closer and you will see a pair of Osprey right in the middle and a lone one on the left.
