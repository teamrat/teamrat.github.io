After placing our tripods in stratgeic location, we rushed to get the sunset view of half dome from the glacier point. I think we missed what we were supposed to get. But I was motivated and I had all this new ideas of framing and exposure fresh in my head so I split from teh grouo to the side framed this view and kept shooting every few seconds.
