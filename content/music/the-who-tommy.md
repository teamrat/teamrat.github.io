Dark gold-and-black Decca rainbow label, original roll-fold/trifold cover, 12-page colour booklet intact. Dead wax 7 11978 3 / 7 11977 10. US release 31 May 1969. Contemporary Billboard coverage describes the booklet, and the discographic record identifies the US Decca issue as the trifold-plus-booklet package.

The package is the collectible part here, more than the pressing — booklets go missing and trifolds split along the folds. Worth an outer sleeve and worth not filing it under weight. Oldest rock original in the collection apart from the Rubber Soul.
