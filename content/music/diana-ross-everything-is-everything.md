Matrix Z4RS-3697 / HS-1797 on side A. RCA mastering, cut by Randy Kling. An RCA Indianapolis pressing is plausible on that evidence, but without the specific plant mark it stays unconfirmed.

I thought this record was scandalous when I was a child in Asmara, on the strength of what Diana Ross is wearing on the cover.

Third Motown original in the collection, after the Jackson 5 Third Album and the Supremes Greatest Hits — and the second record here with Diana Ross on it, three years after the Supremes set.
