US original — APL1 is RCA's single-LP prefix for 1973–76, and Billboard listed it as a new release in May 1975. Produced by Yusuf Rahman. This is Pickett's RCA period, after he left Atlantic, so it isn't the mid-sixties Stax/Muscle Shoals material that carries his reputation — worth knowing if the childhood association is with the earlier sides.
