Spanish public-domain reissue, same family as the Wax Time Moanin'. 'High definition premium' names no engineer, studio or tape source, and the sleeve copy is generic filler about vinyl's popularity rather than anything about this record. The clearest tell is the tracklist: the original Time Out has seven tracks, and this has eight — 'Audrey' is a 1954 Brubeck Time recording, so the source is an assembled digital compilation, not the album master.
