Side A deadwax `BSK 1 3480-E2`, side B `BSK 2 3480-E2`. The matching E2 matrices indicate revised lacquer cuts, consistent with a 1980 US original-release-era pressing.

The first Dire Straits original in the collection. The Brothers in Arms alongside it is a modern reissue of a digitally recorded album, so no vinyl edition of that record can be all-analog. This one can be.

Lands at 1980 with the Devo, where the rock shelf otherwise stops.
