Genuine 1982 US Pitman pressing, not merely an early copy — matrices PAL 38112-2ED / PBL 38112-3D match the documented first-run stampers, with a printed inner sleeve carrying 38112 / QE 38112. Engineered by Bruce Swedien for Quincy Jones, and one of the more carefully made pop records of its era, so an original in good shape is worth having.
