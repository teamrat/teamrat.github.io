Side B `ST-A-804594-A`, with additional deadwax `SP-0-4 A24937` and further cursive markings currently unreadable. `SP` is consistent with Specialty Records Corporation, but a specific plant or pressing variant should not be assigned from `SP-0-4` alone. If it does resolve to Specialty it would be the fourth here, after the two Tom Waits and the Doors club edition.

The SD 16023 US edition and the `ST-A-804594` matrix are independently consistent with the US Atlantic issue. The matrix also sits close to the Phil Collins `ST-A-825051`, two years apart.
