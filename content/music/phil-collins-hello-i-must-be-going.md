Side A deadwax `ST-A-825051-B B-17422-B`. `ST-A-825051` is the documented side A matrix for this album, and `B-17422-B` appears on US copies carrying it. The 80035-1 issue is original-era Atlantic rather than a modern reissue.

The `ST-A-` prefix ties it to Roberta Flack's First Take (`ST-A-69163-H`, 1969) and, through the Rolling Stones Records imprint Atlantic distributed, to Some Girls and Love You Live. Four records sharing one label's matrix system across thirteen years.
