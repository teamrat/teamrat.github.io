Not the ordinary 1970 Elektra first release but an identifiable RCA Music Service club edition with its own mastering and matrix lineage; the A/B combination is documented. Third Specialty pressing of the evening, after the two Waits.

Club editions are usually treated as lesser, and often are — separate lacquers cut for a mail-order run, sometimes from a dub. Worth listening for whether this one differs from the Elektra you'd expect, since you know the material well. A compilation rather than an album.
