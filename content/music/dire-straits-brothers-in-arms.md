2013 reissue. Worth knowing that the 1985 album was one of the first all-digital major recordings — tracked to Sony digital multitrack — so no vinyl edition can be all-analog. Any 'AAA' claim for this title would be wrong at the source. <span class='flag'>unverified</span>

Warner Bros. R1 25264, a recent Warner/Rhino 2-LP reissue on 180g at 33 1/3. Spreading a single album across four sides means wider groove spacing and less inner-groove compromise, which is a real improvement in the cutting. It does nothing about the source: the ceiling was set upstream in 1985.
