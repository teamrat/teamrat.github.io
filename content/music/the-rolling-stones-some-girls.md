Early first-version configuration: the withdrawn die-cut celebrity-face sleeve plus yellow label. Side A stamped ST-RS-784079-MO — MO for Monarch Record Mfg., Los Angeles, with PR in the runout tied to the Presswell lacquer designation. MO alone wouldn't establish a first pressing, but with the die-cut package it places the copy firmly in the early 1978 group. The original sleeve was pulled after objections from the celebrities pictured, which makes the jacket the collectible part — worth an outer sleeve.
