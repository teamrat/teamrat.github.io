The T prefix is Capitol's mono number — stereo was ST 2442. Note that the US Capitol Rubber Soul is a different album from the UK Parlophone: Capitol dropped four tracks and substituted two from Help!, which reshaped it into a folk-rock record. Neither version is a subset of the other.
