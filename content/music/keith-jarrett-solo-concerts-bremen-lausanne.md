Original ECM box set. ECM chose this album to launch its Luminessence audiophile reissue series in 2023 — and this is the original. Production standards in this period were exceptional. Solo piano, so it doubles as the hardest test of turntable speed stability. The Lausanne concert was recorded about five weeks before Teamrat was born.
