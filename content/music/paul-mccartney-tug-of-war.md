Side B `BL 37462-1L`, with the plant mark `1T` / `T` for Columbia's Terre Haute, Indiana plant. Side A read as `AL 37462-1L`, though whether that is the corresponding A-side matrix is not confirmed. The `1L` lacquer is a later revision, but the pressing itself is from the original 1982 release period.

The deadwax carries a hand-etched "Pushing + Pulling" — someone at the lathe answering the album title.

Terre Haute is the first Midwestern plant identified in the collection. The `AL` / `BL` prefixes put it in the same Columbia family as We Are the World and the Epic Thriller. Second Beatle-adjacent record after the mono Capitol Rubber Soul.
