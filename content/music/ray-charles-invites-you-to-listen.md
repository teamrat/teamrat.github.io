Original 1967 release — contemporary trade listings show ABC 595 / ABCS 595 as the new album. Ten standards and ballads, including 'Yesterday' and 'How Deep Is the Ocean,' so this is Charles the interpreter of the songbook rather than the gospel-blues of the Atlantic years. Third Ray Charles in the collection, and with the Tangerine ABCS-695 it brackets his ABC period either side of 1968.
