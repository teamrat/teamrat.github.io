Original US catalogue number and release, 14 June 1972 — a compilation assembled two years after the duo split, not a reissue of an album. Includes live recordings alongside the studio tracks. The KC prefix precedes Columbia's mid-1970s change to PC, the prefix on the Kind of Blue and In a Silent Way copies.
