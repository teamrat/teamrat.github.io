Barcode 081227880385, released 12 November 2021. The catalogue and barcode combination is documented for this edition.

The audio is the 2015 Paisley Park remaster, which makes this a remaster rather than simply a later pressing — a different thing from the MCA Captain Fantastic, where the later pressing came off the original production master.

Two identical copies on the shelf. Catalogued once, since nothing distinguishes them.

Second coloured-vinyl record here after the green Marvin Gaye. Coloured compound runs slightly noisier than black, though on a modern remaster that is not the limiting factor.
