Label reads `MCA-37006 (MCA 734)`; deadwax `MCA 734 W15` with a cursive `B.D.I.`. The original 1975 US LP was MCA-2142, and MCA-37006 is identified as the 1980 US reissue.

The `MCA 734` matrix corresponds to the first side of the earlier MCA-2142 production master, and `B.D.I.` is documented in the runouts of this title. `W15` is a later stamper sequence within that matrix family. Same mastering as the original, later stampers — a reissue rather than a remaster.
