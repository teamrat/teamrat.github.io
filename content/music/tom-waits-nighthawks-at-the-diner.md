Matrices 7E-2008-A(PRC) / -B(PRC), with C and D sides present. PRC operated plants at Compton, California and Richmond, Indiana, so the code alone doesn't fix which. The 7E prefix is already in use here in 1975, so Asylum's change from SD came between 1973 and 1975.

Recorded live in a studio in front of an invited audience — the transitional record between the two others, with the small-group jazz backing already in place and Waits talking between numbers. Third Waits in the collection — only Herbie Mann and Ray Charles are represented as heavily.
