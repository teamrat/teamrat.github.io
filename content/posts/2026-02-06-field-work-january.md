Spent the morning in an almond orchard east of Merced, installing soil moisture sensors. January field work in the Central Valley means mud, fog, and cold fingers. The farmer asked good questions about why we're measuring at 30cm depth instead of 15cm.

<!--more-->

The honest answer is: because that's what the protocol says. But his question is better than our protocol. He knows his trees, knows his irrigation schedule, knows exactly which parts of the field dry out first. We show up with our sensors and our models and act like we're teaching him something.

Sometimes I think we'd learn more if we spent less time deploying instruments and more time listening.
