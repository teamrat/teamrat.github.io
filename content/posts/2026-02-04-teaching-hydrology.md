Every semester I teach soil hydrology to civil engineering students, and every semester the same thing happens: they want equations, I want them to think about water. We eventually meet in the middle, but it takes most of the semester to get there.

This article captures the tension beautifully—the difference between knowing how to calculate infiltration rate and understanding what infiltration actually means in a real landscape.
