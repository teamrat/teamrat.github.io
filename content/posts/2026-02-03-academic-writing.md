I've been editing a student's manuscript and keep finding myself deleting entire paragraphs. Not because they're wrong, but because they say nothing. Academic writing has this terrible tendency toward inflation—using three sentences where one would do, invoking complex frameworks when simple description would be clearer.

<!--more-->

The best science writing I've read does the opposite. It trusts the reader. It uses plain language. It doesn't mistake complexity for sophistication.

My student's paper is good research. Once we strip away the defensive scaffolding, it might even be a good paper.
