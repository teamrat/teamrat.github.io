---
title: "On Tending Soil and Tending Code"
date: 2026-02-06T10:00:00-08:00
tags: ["observation"]
---

There is a deep analogy between cultivating healthy soil and writing good software. Both require patience. Both reward those who understand the underlying structure rather than just the surface. A soil that looks productive can be mining its own carbon reserves — living on borrowed time. Code that ships fast can be accruing technical debt in the same way.

The farmer who builds soil organic matter is playing a long game. So is the developer who writes clean abstractions. Neither gets credit in the short term. Both are doing the most important work.
